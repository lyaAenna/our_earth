Sustainable Practices

Conserving Energy

Turn off the lights when you leave any room for 15 minutes or more. Consider using controls such as timers that automatically turn off lights when you're not using them, and dimmers that use lower energy.

Reducing Waste

Switching from printed materials to digital formats can significantly reduce your carbon footprint. While digital devices do consume energy, reducing physical paper helps save trees and landfill waste.

Using Eco-Friendly Products

Opt for reusable, biodegradable, and non-toxic household products to eliminate single-use plastics and harmful chemicals from entering our local water systems.

Future Goals

  1. Reduce Waste and Plastic Pollution: Encourage people to use reusable bags, bottles, and containers. Promote recycling and reduce single-use plastics to prevent pollution.
  2. Plant More Trees: Organize tree-planting activities to restore forests, improve air quality, and provide habitats for wildlife.
  3. Promote Renewable Energy: Encourage the use of solar, wind, and other renewable energy sources to reduce dependence on fossil fuels.
  4. Increase Environmental Awareness: Educate students and communities about climate change, pollution, and sustainable living through campaigns, workshops, and Earth Day activities.
  5. Encourage Community Participation: Organize community clean-up programmes, recycling campaigns, and environmental volunteering activities.
  6. Protect Wildlife and Natural Habitats: Support conservation programmes, prevent deforestation, and protect endangered species to maintain biodiversity.
