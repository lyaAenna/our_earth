Success Stories & Impact

Success Stories #1

Reforestation & Indigenous Health: Health in Harmony (Borneo)

The Core Problem: Tropical rainforest destruction is deeply tied to poverty and healthcare access. When families cannot afford medical emergencies, they often turn to illegal logging to pay for healthcare[cite: 2].

The Innovative Solution: Health In Harmony uses a planetary health approach centered on radical listening—asking Indigenous peoples and forest-guarding communities what they need to protect their environment. Crucially, the model is driven by local women[cite: 2].

Measurable Results (Indonesian Borneo Pilot):

  • 90% drop in households participating in illegal logging[cite: 2].
  • 67% decrease in infant mortality[cite: 2].
  • Stabilization of primary forest loss alongside 21,000 hectares of secondary forest regrowth[cite: 2].

Success Stories #2

Coral Restoration at Summer Bay Resort & Coralku

The Core Challenge: Vital coral reefs around Lang Tengah Island face severe threats from environmental degradation, heavy sedimentation, and storm damage[cite: 2].

The Innovative Solution: Summer Bay Resort partnered with non-profit marine conservation organization Coralku using durable, low-cost metal frames as artificial nurseries to rescue broken corals[cite: 2].

Key Milestones & Impact: Deployment plan aiming for at least 100 coral frames carrying roughly 5,000 pieces of broken coral, leading to natural spawning of 8 different coral species[cite: 2].

Success Stories #3

Deforestation Control: The MSPO & NDPE Policy

The Core Context: Balancing rapid national economic development with rainforest preservation in resource-rich nations like Malaysia[cite: 2].

Key Findings: Slowing plantation expansion for commodities like oil palm and rubber, coupled with zero-deforestation supply chains and stronger policy frameworks safeguarding primary forest cover[cite: 2].
